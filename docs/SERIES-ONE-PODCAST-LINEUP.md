# Series One Podcast Lineup

Series One uses a fortnightly release cadence after the first Spotify episode.
Each episode should remain useful as a standalone listen while building a larger
arc from personal reliance to institutional control.

## Release Pattern

- Cadence: every other Thursday during the series
- Format: scripted roundtable adapted for audio
- Season size: six episodes
- Production rule: keep at least one finished or near-finished episode in reserve
- Public expectation: "New episodes every other Thursday during each series"

## Arc Notes

Episode 2 should follow directly from the trust episode. Episode 1 gives the
listener a personal decision rule; Episode 2 asks what happens when personal
verification is not enough because the system is embedded in a company,
government service, employer, school, or healthcare workflow.

Episode 3 can reuse the earlier explanation topic, but it should be rerun rather
than simply converted to audio. The Series One version should use the approved
model slate, visible moderator turns, podcast-adaptable closing synthesis, and a
listener-first title.

Episodes 4-6 should move from individual use to systems and power: personal
data, skill dependency, and institutional control.
