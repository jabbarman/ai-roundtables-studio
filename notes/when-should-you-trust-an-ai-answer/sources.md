# Source Packet: When Should You Trust an AI Answer?

These sources establish practical risks and governance expectations. They do
not dictate a single trust rule.

## NIST Generative AI Profile

- URL: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence
- Relevance: Identifies confabulation, information integrity, human over-reliance,
  and evaluation as connected risks requiring controls beyond model fluency.

## Generative AI Framework for UK Government

- URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/generative-ai-framework-for-hmg/generative-ai-framework-for-hmg-html
- Relevance: Treats generated material as something people and organisations
  must verify, govern, and use with proportionate human oversight.

## WHO Guidance on Large Multi-Modal Models

- URL: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240084759
- Relevance: Shows why plausible output requires stronger evidence and oversight
  when health decisions and unequal access to expertise are involved.

## FTC: Keep Your AI Claims in Check

- URL: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check
- Relevance: Challenges unsupported claims about AI capability and accuracy,
  shifting some responsibility from individual users to product makers.
