# Source Packet: What Should AI Be Allowed to Know About You?

These sources support a discussion about personalisation, data minimisation,
sensitive inference, retention, and meaningful user control. They should inform
practical distinctions rather than turn the episode into legal advice.

## NIST Privacy Framework

- URL: https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework
- Relevance: Frames privacy risk as problems people may experience because of
  data processing across the full data lifecycle, and provides a structure for
  governing, controlling, and communicating those risks.

## ICO: Security and Data Minimisation in AI

- URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/how-should-we-assess-security-and-data-minimisation-in-ai/
- Relevance: States that AI systems should process only the personal data needed
  for a defined purpose, including at inference time, rather than collecting
  information because it might become useful later.

## ICO: Lawfulness in AI

- URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/how-do-we-ensure-lawfulness-in-ai/
- Relevance: Addresses lawful processing and the status of AI-driven inferences,
  including inferences that may reveal sensitive categories of personal data.

## ICO: Accuracy and Statistical Accuracy

- URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/what-do-we-need-to-know-about-accuracy-and-statistical-accuracy/
- Relevance: Treats inferences about people as personal data whose possible
  inaccuracy and impact must be considered, corrected, and governed.

## OpenAI Memory FAQ

- URL: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-personalization-and-data-controls-faq
- Relevance: Provides a concrete consumer-product example of saved memories,
  chat-history reference, temporary chats, review and deletion controls, and the
  distinction between deleting a chat and deleting a stored memory.
