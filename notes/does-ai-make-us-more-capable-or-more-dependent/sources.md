# Source Packet: Does AI Make Us More Capable or More Dependent?

These sources support a discussion about immediate performance, durable
learning, critical judgment, uneven AI capability, and human agency. The
episode should distinguish evidence from different settings rather than assume
that a result from school, customer support, or consulting applies everywhere.

## Generative AI at Work

- URL: https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161
- Relevance: A study of 5,179 customer-support agents found that AI assistance
  increased issues resolved per hour, with especially large gains among novice
  and lower-skilled workers. It offers evidence that AI can spread established
  expert practices and accelerate performance.

## Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier

- URL: https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838
- Relevance: A preregistered experiment with 758 consultants found gains in
  speed, completion, and quality for tasks within the tested AI capability
  frontier, but lower correctness on a task outside it. It makes the ability to
  recognise task boundaries central to useful augmentation.

## The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking

- URL: https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713778
- Relevance: A CHI 2025 survey of 319 knowledge workers found that greater
  confidence in generative AI was associated with less reported critical
  thinking, while greater self-confidence was associated with more. It also
  describes a shift from direct execution toward verification, integration, and
  stewardship.

## Generative AI Without Guardrails Can Harm Learning

- URL: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122
- Relevance: A field experiment with nearly one thousand high-school maths
  students found that answer-giving AI improved assisted practice but could
  weaken unaided performance, while a tutor designed to give hints mitigated the
  harm. Tool design changes whether assistance substitutes for or supports
  learning.

## UNESCO Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research

- URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research
- Relevance: Provides a human-centred framework that prioritises human agency,
  foundational skills, appropriate use, and pedagogical design rather than
  adopting generative AI solely for immediate output.
