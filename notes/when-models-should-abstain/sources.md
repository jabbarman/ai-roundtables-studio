# Source Packet: When Models Should Abstain

These sources are meant to ground the discussion, not dictate the conclusions.

## NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0

- URL: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
- Kind: framework
- Relevance: Frames trustworthy AI around characteristics such as validity, reliability, safety, security, transparency, explainability, privacy, and fairness. Useful for asking whether abstention is a reliability behavior, a safety behavior, or a user-trust behavior.

## OpenAI Model Spec

- URL: https://cdn.openai.com/spec/model-spec-2024-05-08.html
- Kind: model behavior spec
- Relevance: Includes behavior guidance around uncertainty, refusals, and assistant helpfulness. Useful for distinguishing refusal from calibrated assistance.

## Anthropic Responsible Scaling Policy

- URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/responsible-scaling-policy-v3
- Kind: policy
- Relevance: Emphasizes risk governance for increasingly capable systems. Useful for connecting individual answers to broader deployment risk.

## Google AI Principles

- URL: https://blog.google/technology/ai/ai-principles/
- Kind: principles
- Relevance: States commitments such as social benefit, avoiding unfair bias, safety, accountability, and privacy. Useful for comparing value-level principles with operational answer policies.
